Privacy Policy

Wardrobe by Wiebracht respects your privacy. This policy explains the limited information collected through this website and how it is used.

Information you provide

When you submit the consultation form, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, and information about the wardrobe assistance you are seeking.

How information is used

Information submitted through the website is used to respond to your inquiry, arrange a consultation, communicate about requested services, and maintain appropriate business records.

Service providers

The consultation form is processed by Formspree, which receives the information you submit and delivers it to Wardrobe by Wiebracht. Website hosting and security providers may also process limited technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to operate and protect the site.

Embedded video and external websites

This website includes an embedded YouTube video and links to third-party websites, including Tom James and social media pages. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and may collect information when you interact with them.

Cookies and analytics

Wardrobe by Wiebracht does not currently use its own advertising cookies or behavioral analytics. Embedded content and third-party links may use cookies or similar technologies according to the policies of those providers.

Sharing and selling information

Wardrobe by Wiebracht does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the website, respond to an inquiry, comply with law, or protect legal rights and site security.

Retention and security

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for communication, client service, business records, legal obligations, and security. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you previously submitted, subject to applicable legal and business-record requirements.

Children

This website is intended for adults seeking professional wardrobe services and is not directed to children under 13.

Updates to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website or its service providers change. The effective date below will be revised when material changes are made.
